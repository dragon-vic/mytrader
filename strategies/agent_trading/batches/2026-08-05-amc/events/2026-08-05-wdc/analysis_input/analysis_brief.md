### First-package extraction

Extract from the first complete official release and simultaneously posted earnings deck:

- FY26 Q4 continuing-operations revenue in US$ billions, non-GAAP gross margin in percentage points, and WDC-defined non-GAAP diluted EPS. Do not use GAAP EPS, which can contain retained-Sandisk and financing marks.
- FY27 Q1 revenue, non-GAAP gross-margin and non-GAAP diluted-EPS guidance. For ranges, calculate midpoint as `(low + high) / 2`; use a disclosed point unchanged.
- The fiscal weeks and period endpoints for both quarters. Q4 must be the 13 weeks ended 2026-07-03. Do not score raw Q1 year-over-year growth because Q1FY26 had 14 weeks.
- The diluted-share convention and any capped-call adjustment used in Q4 EPS and Q1 EPS guidance. Q1 EPS is eligible only if it matches WDC's disclosed non-GAAP convention; otherwise use gross margin as the outlook corroborator.
- Q4 total and nearline exabytes, cloud revenue share, latest-generation drive shipments, and any comparable historical table. These are confirmation fields, not required gates.

### Locked comparisons and calculations

Q4 neutral ranges are revenue $3.69B–$3.71B, gross margin 51.0%–52.0%, and EPS $3.29–$3.35. Material thresholds are $3.79B/$3.62B for revenue, 52.5%/50.5% for gross margin, and $3.59/$3.06 for EPS, with the first value positive and second negative.

FY27 Q1 revenue is compared with $4.02B: $3.98B–$4.06B is neutral, while $4.10B/$3.94B is materially positive/negative. A positive outlook confirmation is WDC-defined EPS at least $3.73 or gross margin at least 52.0% and no more than 0.5 percentage point below Q4. A negative confirmation is EPS at most $3.50, gross margin below 51.0%, or a gross-margin decline exceeding 1.0 percentage point. Material confirmation thresholds are EPS $3.85/$3.30 or gross margin 52.5%/50.5%, with a flat-or-better margin required for the positive case and a decline of at least 1.5 points sufficient for the negative case.

Optional operating calculations are `Q4 total EB / 222 − 1` and `Q4 nearline EB / 199 − 1`; report them as percentages. Resolve any restated category definition before calculating.

Comparison order: apply definition, period and completeness vetoes; test for material cross-sign conflict; then evaluate the broadest tier before progressively weaker tiers. An isolated signal, balanced opposing votes, or an ordinary package inside the neutral bands remains HOLD. Classification is impossible if Q4 revenue, gross margin or WDC-defined EPS is absent, if Q1 revenue is absent, or if the only needed outlook corroborator has an unresolved definition.

### Event-driven focus

- **Orders, pricing and nearline mix — confirmation.** Find the EB table plus exact sentences about pricing, firm purchase orders, LTAs, capacity allocation and cancellations. Rising nearline/total EB with stable or improving pricing supports the financial sign; volume growth attributed only to shipment timing weakens it. A disclosed cancellation or repricing falsifies the demand thesis.
- **40TB and HAMR execution — confirmation or HOLD.** Find an explicit qualification, customer-count or production-timing update against 40TB volume in calendar H2 2026 and HAMR qualification/ramp in 2027 [S13,S14]. On-time milestones confirm durability. A material delay weakens it; a quantified effect excluded from guidance requires HOLD.
- **Customer concentration — possible HOLD.** Look for a sentence or table identifying a major customer loss, order deferral, concentration change or renegotiation and any revenue/margin amount [S4]. Stable or expanding commitments confirm visibility. An unquantified material change keeps the result at HOLD; a quantified change is already represented only if the package explicitly says it is included in guidance.
- **Capacity, yields and supply — confirmation or HOLD.** Locate capex guidance, yield/capacity commentary, supplier constraints and outages; compare capex direction with the prior 4%–6% of revenue framework [S14]. Disciplined capacity and stable yields confirm margin quality. A material unquantified constraint or outage forces HOLD.
- **EPS and separation accounting — HOLD control.** Check the non-GAAP reconciliation, diluted shares, capped-call treatment, retained-Sandisk marks and financing items. A clean bridge confirms EPS usability. An unresolved convention conflict or an item outside guidance large enough to affect Q1 by at least $100M revenue, 50 bp gross margin or $0.10 EPS requires HOLD.
