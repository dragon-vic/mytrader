### Extract from the first complete official Q2 2026 release/8-K

Use the three months ended June 30, 2026 and USD millions unless stated otherwise:

- Reserve income (`RI`), Other Revenue (`O`), and reported Total Revenue and Reserve Income (`T_reported`).
- Any Arc-token revenue, gain, fair-value effect, incentive, or associated cost included in those figures; obtain the numerical ex-Arc bridge.
- Total Distribution, Transaction and Other Costs (`C`).
- Reported RLDC and RLDC margin.
- Adjusted Operating Expense.
- Adjusted EBITDA (`A`), its GAAP reconciliation, exclusions, and stated margin denominator.
- Average daily and June 30 USDC circulation; average reserve assets if separately given; Reserve Return Rate and its period convention.
- FY2026 guidance, on a like-for-like ex-Arc basis, for Other Revenue, RLDC margin, Adjusted Operating Expense, and the through-cycle USDC CAGR objective.
- Any reserve deficiency/depeg, attestation or counterparty problem, material security incident, partner termination, binding regulatory restriction, restatement, or release/8-K conflict.

### Locked definitions and baselines

- `T = T_reported − separately quantified Arc operating revenue/gains`; if no Arc item entered the top line, `T=T_reported`.
- `L = T − C`; `M = 100 × L / T`.
- `A` must use the Q1 2026 definition, including the payroll-tax-on-stock-compensation exclusion, with material Arc items removed.
- `AM = 100 × A / L`; do not divide by total revenue.
- Neutral mechanical ranges: average USDC $75.8–76.8B; June 30 USDC approximately $73.269B; `RI` $666–685M; `O` $38–43M; `T` $708–728M; `L` $269–291M; `M` 38–40%; `A` $125–143M; `AM` approximately 46–50%.
- Prior FY2026 guidance: Other Revenue $150–170M; RLDC margin 38–40%; Adjusted Operating Expense $570–585M; USDC approximately 40% multi-year through-cycle CAGR. Reaffirmation or silence is neutral; compare revised range endpoints and midpoints on the old ex-Arc scope.

### Calculations and comparison order

1. Apply integrity/veto checks and reconcile release versus 8-K.
2. Remove material Arc effects and verify the current non-GAAP definition.
3. Recalculate `L`, `M`, and `AM`; compare reported values with the calculations allowing only rounding.
4. If average reserve assets and the issuer’s annualization convention are disclosed, recompute implied reserve income from average reserve assets × Reserve Return Rate × period fraction. Do not substitute average USDC circulation for average reserve assets.
5. Compare operating engine first, then retained economics, then adjusted profit, and finally like-for-like guidance. Treat EPS, GAAP net income, end-period USDC alone, qualitative partnerships, CPN/Arc milestones, and later call commentary as non-gating.

Classification is impossible if `T`, `C`, current-definition `A`, or the adjusted-EBITDA reconciliation is absent; if material Arc effects cannot be separated; if a same-definition June 30 USDC figure differs from $73.269B by more than $0.5B without a bridge; or if an issuer-input reserve-income identity differs by more than 3% without explanation. Missing RI/O split makes the engine unavailable but does not by itself invalidate independently complete retained-economics and profit evidence. Missing guidance is neutral for weaker classifications; optional platform metrics and later-filing details never gate them.
