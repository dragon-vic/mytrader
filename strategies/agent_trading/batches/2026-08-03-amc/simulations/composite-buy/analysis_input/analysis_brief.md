PLTR Q2 2026 — first-minute operating guide

Use only the first complete official disclosure package. Do not use public consensus or EPS as a trigger, do not inspect price/K-lines, and do not wait for a call, later filing, or commentary.

1. Extract exact fields

- Package timestamp, reporting period, currency/units, and metric definitions.
- Q2 total revenue.
- Q2 U.S. commercial and U.S. government revenue, including official YoY growth.
- GAAP operating income and margin.
- Adjusted operating income (AOI), adjusted margin, and the GAAP reconciliation.
- CFO, capex, adjusted FCF, and its reconciliation.
- Q3 revenue and AOI guidance ranges.
- FY26 revenue and AOI guidance ranges.
- FY26 U.S. commercial revenue amount/growth floor.
- FY26 adjusted-FCF guidance range.
- If present only: U.S. commercial TCV, U.S. commercial RDV, deal counts, RPO, customer/NDR, cancellations, collectibility, or restatement facts.

Calculate:
delta_h2_revenue = midpoint(new FY revenue guide) - 7.656 - (Q2 revenue - 1.799)
delta_h2_aoi = midpoint(new FY AOI guide) - 4.446 - (Q2 AOI - 1.065)
Above +0.001 is positive; below -0.001 is negative; otherwise neutral. If the new range is unbounded or not definition-comparable, HOLD.

3. Classification prerequisites

- Revenue, AOI, segment, GAAP-margin, cash, and guidance values must use comparable definitions and periods.
- A missing mandatory field, an unbounded range that prevents a midpoint, or an unusable GAAP/non-GAAP reconciliation makes directional classification impossible.
- Missing TCV, RDV, deal count, customer count, NDR, or RPO is neutral; extract those fields only when present.
- Record any disclosed restatement, material revenue-recognition/collectibility issue, material cancellation, or explicit one-time timing/reclassification item before applying `research.json` outcomes.
