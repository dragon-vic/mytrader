# SMCI fiscal Q4 2026 pre-event memo — August 11, 2026

## Thesis and the true bar

This is now a forward-guidance, order-quality and cash-conversion event—not a Q4 headline event. On July 21, Supermicro already disclosed revenue near the bottom of its prior $11.0–12.5B range, 15–17% GAAP and adjusted gross margin versus prior 8.2–8.4% guidance, and more than $60B of new Q4 orders. Crucially, it did **not** disclose ending backlog dollars and warned that some orders may be non-firm, delayed or cancelled. It also said the export-control review could affect forecasts or reported periods. ([Company update, July 21](https://ir.supermicro.com/news/news-details/2026/Supermicro-Provides-Fourth-Quarter-of-Fiscal-Year-2026-Preliminary-Business-Update/default.aspx))

The market’s real hurdle is roughly:

- FY27 revenue around $56B or better, versus published consensus near $53B;
- a credible Q1 run-rate around $13–13.5B or higher, versus consensus near $11.8B;
- sustainable gross margin near 11% or better—not necessarily Q4’s exceptional 16%;
- evidence that orders are firm, diversified and deliverable without another working-capital or dilution shock.

Guidance around $53–55B without margin, conversion and cash detail would mostly confirm expectations already reset after the preview.

## Expectations and estimate conflicts

**Reported Q4 expectation.** Management’s wording implies revenue of approximately **$11.0–11.3B** and FY26 revenue of $38.9–39.2B. Current published estimates around $11.55–11.60B appear insufficiently updated. Likewise, current adjusted-EPS aggregations vary from $0.92–0.96 to roughly $1.33. ([Visible Alpha, August 10](https://www.investopedia.com/here-is-how-much-super-micro-computer-stock-is-expected-to-move-after-earnings-smci-q4-fy2026-12036777); [August 11 analyst preview](https://www.tipranks.com/news/last-minute-thought-barclays-weighs-in-on-smci-stock-ahead-of-earnings))

My model uses management’s May operating-cost, tax and diluted-share assumptions, adjusted for June financing. At $11.0–11.3B revenue and 15–17% margin, the economically coherent range is approximately **$1.40–1.55 adjusted EPS**, with about $1.30–1.35 a reasonable observable market bar if legal, compliance or closing expenses rose. The old $0.92 estimate is inconsistent with the preannounced gross-profit pool absent a large undisclosed expense. ([May prepared remarks](https://s204.q4cdn.com/707617056/files/doc_financials/2026/q3/SMCI-Q326-Prepared-Remarks.pdf))

**Forward expectation.** Current FY27 consensus is approximately **$53.0–53.3B revenue and $3.24–3.31 adjusted EPS**. ([FactSet estimates](https://www.finanzen.net/schaetzungen/super_micro); [S&P Global aggregation, July 29](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/smci/forecast/)) My range is **$54–58B and $3.7–4.5**, based on 10.5–12% normalized gross margin, enlarged dilution and only partial FY27 conversion of the order intake. I would not annualize Q4’s 16%.

## What can change the view

The $60B claim is less firm than headlines imply. June’s approximately $39B of recent orders from more than 20 customers was received during Q4 and is almost certainly largely included—not additive. The financing documents say that cohort did not constitute firm commitments and could be cancelled or delayed. There is no reconciliation for cancellations, platform rebookings, deposits or recognition timing. ([June financing terms](https://ir.supermicro.com/news/news-details/2026/Supermicro-Announces-Pricing-of-Equity-and-Equity-Linked-Financing-Transactions-To-Fund-AI-Orders/default.aspx); [preferred prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1375365/000119312526268520/d44171d424b5.htm))

Margin is highly customer-mix sensitive. One customer generated roughly 63% of Q2 revenue, when adjusted gross margin fell to 6.4%; concentration dropped to 27% in Q3 and margin recovered to 10.1%. Q4’s 15–17% is credible for the completed quarter, but Supermicro has not quantified DCBBS revenue, attach rate or profit contribution sufficiently to make it a durable baseline. ([Q2 remarks](https://s204.q4cdn.com/707617056/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/SMCI-Q226-Prepared-Remarks-UPDATED.pdf); [Q3 remarks](https://s204.q4cdn.com/707617056/files/doc_financials/2026/q3/SMCI-Q326-Prepared-Remarks.pdf))

Demand itself is not the disputed fact: NVIDIA’s latest data-center revenue rose 92% year over year, while Dell booked $24.4B of AI orders and recognized $16.1B of AI-server revenue in its May quarter. But NVIDIA’s Rubin ecosystem also includes Dell, HPE, Lenovo and several direct ODMs. SMCI creates excess value only if its rapid integration, liquid cooling and DCBBS content produce profitable share—not merely high-volume GPU pass-through. ([NVIDIA Q1 FY27](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-first-quarter-fiscal-2027); [Dell Q1 FY27](https://investors.delltechnologies.com/news-releases/news-release-details/dell-technologies-delivers-first-quarter-fiscal-2027-financial); [Rubin ecosystem](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/vera-rubin-full-production-agentic-ai-factory))

Cash is the strongest falsification point. At March 31, cash was $1.29B against about $8.8B of debt, inventory was $11.1B, and nine-month operating cash flow was negative $7.56B. June’s base offerings raised approximately $4.9B net; the advertised $7B includes unconfirmed underwriter options and the full $1.25B ATM. The common and mandatory-convertible preferred financing represents eventual dilution of roughly 23–26% before any ATM, while the preferred carries $262.5M of annual dividends. ([Q3 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1375365/000137536526000014/smci-20260331.htm); [financing release](https://ir.supermicro.com/news/news-details/2026/Supermicro-Announces-Pricing-of-Equity-and-Equity-Linked-Financing-Transactions-To-Fund-AI-Orders/default.aspx))

## Directional framework

A simple confirmation of $11.0–11.3B revenue, 15–17% margin and the existing order language has an ordinary region around **-4% to +4%**, with modest sell-the-news skew.

Credible FY27 conversion—roughly $56B or more, double-digit durable margin, a strong Q1 start, quantified firm backlog and improving working capital—supports **+12% to +22%**, with a stronger short-covering tail toward 25–30%.

Margin reversion, FY27 around or below $52–53B, Q1 near or below $12B, delayed/conditional orders or renewed capital needs supports **-16% to -25%**. An adverse export-review finding or revision to preliminary/prior results could extend toward **-25% to -35%**.

The expected complete absolute repricing is approximately **10–13%**, with a plausible non-tail envelope near **-18% to +20%**. The largest unresolved uncertainty is not demand; it is whether Supermicro can turn unusually soft, capital-intensive orders into durable per-share earnings.
