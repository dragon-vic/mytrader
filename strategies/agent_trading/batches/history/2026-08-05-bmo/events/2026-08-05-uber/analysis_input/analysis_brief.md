### Extract from the first complete official package

- Release/8-K publication timestamp, version, and whether figures are final or corrected.
- Q2 total Gross Bookings; reported and constant-currency YoY growth; disclosed FX effect; Mobility, Delivery and Freight Gross Bookings and growth; Trips and MAPCs.
- Q2 issuer-defined non-GAAP diluted EPS, weighted-average diluted shares, Adjusted EBITDA, non-GAAP operating income, corresponding reconciliation items, free cash flow and revenue. Record the quantified UK presentation effect separately.
- Q3 Gross Bookings range and constant-currency midpoint, FX assumption, Adjusted EBITDA range or translation, non-GAAP EPS range, and every acquisition/divestiture scope adjustment.
- Any change to Delivery Hero consideration, financing, leverage, closing timetable or accretion; material equity issuance/buyback change; AV commitment; legal settlement; or other quantified cash/value item.

### Locked definitions and baselines

- Q2 2026 issuer guide: Gross Bookings $56.25–57.75B; constant-currency growth 18–22%; approximately 2pp reported-growth FX benefit; non-GAAP EPS $0.78–0.82; Adjusted EBITDA $2.70–2.80B.
- Q2 2025: Gross Bookings $46.756B; Adjusted EBITDA $2.119B. Q3 2025: Gross Bookings $49.740B; Adjusted EBITDA $2.256B.
- Gross Bookings includes Mobility/Delivery consumer spend and Freight revenue, including taxes, tolls and fees but excluding tips. Constant currency uses prior-period monthly exchange rates. MAPCs are average monthly unique transactors; Trips are completed rides/orders.
- Use Uber's stated non-GAAP definitions and same-scope, same-period amounts. Do not substitute GAAP EPS or a vendor-adjusted EPS definition.
- Delivery Hero baseline: €41.50/share, approximately $14.8B total equity value, roughly €14B bridge, gross leverage below 2x, accretive upon close/high-single-digit percentage by year three, H2 2027 closing.

### Calculations and comparison order

1. Validate period, scope, definitions and reconciliations against the prior-year bases.
2. Calculate reported Gross Bookings growth as Q2 Gross Bookings / $46.756B − 1; compare it with issuer-reported constant-currency growth and the disclosed FX bridge.
3. Calculate Q3 range midpoints. Calculate Q3 EBITDA growth as midpoint / $2.256B − 1.
4. Calculate non-GAAP operating margin as non-GAAP operating income / Gross Bookings and, if a recast Q2 2025 value is supplied, its YoY growth and margin change in basis points.
5. Compare in this order: issuer definitions and guide ranges; definition-matched public benchmarks; segment/Trips/MAPCs coherence; revenue and GAAP reconciliation diagnostics; quantified corporate-term changes.

### Missing facts that make classification impossible

- No complete official release or filing, an expressly preliminary package, or unresolved conflicting/corrected figures.
- Missing Q2 total Gross Bookings, or both Q2 issuer-defined non-GAAP EPS and Adjusted EBITDA missing.
- A material Q2 definition, consolidation or scope change without a like-for-like bridge when it could change the comparison.
- Explicit guidance withdrawal/suspension because of material uncertainty, rather than ordinary omission.
- A material corporate-term change whose economic sign cannot be uniquely determined. Mere absence of Q3 guidance is recorded as unavailable and does not by itself prevent classification.
