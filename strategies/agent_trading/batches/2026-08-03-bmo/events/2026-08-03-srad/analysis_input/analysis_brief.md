### Trigger and scope
Use only the first complete official Q2 2026 earnings release or its attached 6-K. Confirm that it covers the three and six months ended June 30, 2026 and reports in euros. Do not use the earnings call or a later filing.

### Exact fields to extract
- Q2 and H1 total revenue, in €000.
- Q2 and H1 Sportradar-defined Adjusted EBITDA, in €000, and disclosed Adjusted EBITDA margin.
- Q2 revenue for Betting & Gaming Content, Managed Betting Services, Betting Technology & Solutions, Sports Content, Technology & Services, Rest of World and United States, with the stated YoY rates.
- FY2026 reported-euro revenue and Adjusted EBITDA ranges, or an explicit statement that each was reiterated.
- FY2026 constant-currency revenue-growth and Adjusted-EBITDA-growth ranges, margin-expansion target and FCF-conversion target.
- Any definition or scope change involving IMG ARENA, sport-right amortization, constant currency or Customer Net Retention Rate.
- NRR and FCF only if present; record their stated scope but treat omission as neutral.

### Locked baselines and definitions
- Q2 consensus: revenue €383.8m; Adjusted EBITDA €79.5m; implied margin 20.71%.
- Q2 2025: revenue €317.790m; Adjusted EBITDA €63.838m; margin 20.1%.
- Q1 2026 checksum amounts: revenue €346.518m; Adjusted EBITDA €66.006m.
- H1 2025: revenue €629.021m; Adjusted EBITDA €122.793m; margin 19.5%.
- Prior FY2026 reported guidance: revenue €1,557–1,582m and Adjusted EBITDA €390–400m.
- Prior FY2026 constant-currency guidance: revenue growth 23–25% and Adjusted EBITDA growth 34–37%; margin expansion 200–225bp; FCF conversion above 56%.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin = Adjusted EBITDA ÷ total revenue × 100. Adjusted EBITDA includes the full economic cost of sport rights, including amortization of capitalized rights.

### Locked calculations
1. Revenue surprise % = (Q2 revenue ÷ €383.8m − 1) × 100.
2. Adjusted EBITDA surprise % = (Q2 Adjusted EBITDA ÷ €79.5m − 1) × 100.
3. Recalculate Q2 margin and compare it with 20.71% consensus and 20.1% YoY.
4. Check H1 revenue = Q2 revenue + €346.518m and H1 Adjusted EBITDA = Q2 Adjusted EBITDA + €66.006m.
5. Calculate Q2 YoY growth from the official 2025 comparatives; do not describe it as organic unless the issuer explicitly supplies an ex-IMG measure.
6. For each guidance field, compare lower bound, midpoint and upper bound with the locked prior range. Compare reported-euro ranges only with reported-euro ranges and constant-currency percentages only with constant-currency percentages.
7. At unchanged guidance, calculate the H2 revenue and Adjusted EBITDA required to reach each FY endpoint and the implied H2 margin.

### Comparison order and impossible cases
Authenticate the complete package first; then verify period, currency, units and definitions; compare FY guidance; compare Q2 revenue, Adjusted EBITDA and margin; finally use product, geography, NRR and FCF as confirmation. Calculation is impossible if total revenue or issuer-defined Adjusted EBITDA is missing, FY guidance status is ambiguous, the package is partial, currency/period/scope cannot be reconciled, or the Adjusted EBITDA/sport-right definition changes without a bridge. Missing segment, geography, NRR, FCF or quantified IMG detail alone does not make the primary calculations impossible.
